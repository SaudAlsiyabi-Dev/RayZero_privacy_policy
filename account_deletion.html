Delete your Squash data

App: Squash (Android package com.rayzero.squash)
Developer: Ray Zero
Contact: saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com

You do not have to open the game or install it again. Send us an e-mail and we delete the data. A parent or guardian can ask for a child.

E-mail us to delete data

How to ask

Write to saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com with the subject Delete Squash data and tell us:

  1. Which data you want removed: the online account (sign-in ID and password), the leaderboard scores, or both.
  2. The ID:
    • for the online account, the sign-in ID that starts with SQ- (in the game: Profile > Account);
    • for the leaderboard scores, the Online ID shown at the bottom of the leaderboards screen.
  3. Nothing else is needed. Please do not send passwords, real names or photos.

The game does not know names or e-mail addresses, so we cannot find your data without the ID.

What happens next

  • We delete the account and/or the leaderboard scores that the ID points to, and we confirm by e-mail within 30 days.
  • We keep only the date of your request and the word "deleted". Nothing else about the player is kept by us.
  • Google and Unity keep what they collect under their own policies. For the data Unity holds about Squash players we ask Unity to delete it.

What is deleted, and what is not

DataWhat we do
Online account: sign-in ID and password (Unity Authentication)Deleted for good.
Leaderboard scores, the made-up name, picture, frame and level (Google Firebase)Deleted when you ask for it (see above).
Game progress on the phone (levels, coins, pets)We never receive it. Uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone’s settings to remove it. Android may keep a copy in the Google backup of the phone; the phone’s backup settings control that.
PurchasesHandled by Google Play. We cannot delete store records; Google keeps them for payments, taxes and refunds.
Reward video dataHeld by Google AdMob under Google’s policy. We do not receive it.

Delete the account inside the game

Open Squash > Profile > Account > Delete account. A grown-up question comes first. This removes the online account (the ID and the password). To remove the leaderboard scores too, send us the request above. You can also switch the online leaderboards off in Settings: then nothing more is sent.

Our full privacy policy is here: https://saudalsiyabi-dev.github.io/RayZero_privacy_policy/
