Squash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026
App: Squash (Android package com.rayzero.squash)
Developer: Ray Zero
Contact: saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com | Website: https://saudalsiyabi-dev.github.io/RayZero_privacy_policy/

The short version

  • Squash is made for children, pre-teens and teens. We treat every player as a child.
  • The game does not ask for a real name, an e-mail address, a phone number, a photo or a location.
  • Nothing a player types is shown to other players. There is no chat.
  • Online leaderboards and sign-in accounts are OFF until a grown-up turns them on.
  • The only ads are optional reward videos. They are made for children and are not personalised. The game does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete online data at any time: see "Deleting data".

1. What Squash is

Squash is a casual game in which you squash cartoon flies and other bugs, beat bosses, raise a pet, dress it up and answer quiz questions in a mode called Brain Swarm. It is made for children, pre-teens and teens (about 6 to 15 years old). Squash is free to download. Some things can be bought with real money, and players can choose to watch reward videos.

2. Children

  • We treat every player as a child. Ads are requested as child-directed, with the lowest ("G") content rating, and are not personalised. The game does not ask for or use the Android advertising ID.
  • A grown-up question (a times-table problem) comes before: buying something with real money, watching a reward video, opening a web page, creating or using an account, and turning on the online leaderboards.
  • We do not knowingly ask children for personal information such as a real name, address, e-mail address or phone number, and the game has no place to type one. Player names are made by the game from friendly words (for example "BraveComet42").
  • Some laws count technical identifiers as personal information. The online features below use such identifiers, and that is why a grown-up must switch them on.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and think we hold something about your child, write to saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com and see "Deleting data".

3. What stays on the phone

Game progress is saved on the device: levels, stars, coins, diamonds, pets and outfits, settings, what you own, the made-up player name and the made-up player ID (it looks like "SQ-7KQ2MX"), and the list of purchases. We do not receive this. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Android may back up the game's files to the Google account of the phone (Android Auto Backup) and restore them on a new phone. The phone's backup settings control that, and we do not receive that copy.

5. Ads

  • The only ads are optional reward videos ("Free Rewards" in the menu). A player chooses to watch one and then gets coins or a diamond.
  • There are no ads when the game starts, between levels, when a game ends, or during a round of Brain Swarm.
  • Ads are requested for children, with the lowest content rating, and with personalised advertising turned off.

6. Purchases

Purchases cost real money and go through Google Play (or the Apple App Store, if it is added later). A grown-up question comes first. The items are diamond packs, a Mega Deal bundle and the Royal Pass (a season pass). Random rewards that cost diamonds or coins show their chances next to the button. Receipts and payment details are handled by the store.

7. Online leaderboards and accounts (optional)

  • Leaderboards are off until a grown-up turns them on (Settings > Online leaderboards, or the "Ask a grown-up" button on the leaderboards screen). They can be turned off again at any time, and then nothing is sent. Other players can see the made-up name, the picture, the frame, the player level and the score. Nothing else.
  • Accounts are optional. An account keeps the sign-in ID and a password online so the same ID works on another phone. We never see the password, because Unity stores it protected. The game progress is not copied to the account.
  • Players cannot type anything that other players can read. Names, pets and pictures come from lists in the game.

8. How long we keep data

  • Data on the phone: until the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
  • Accounts: until they are deleted.
  • Leaderboard entries: until the board is reset (the Daily Rush board resets every day), until you ask us to remove them, or until the entry is replaced by a newer one.
  • Purchase records stay with Google Play and Unity as long as they need them for payments, taxes and refunds.
  • Messages you send us about deletion: we keep only the date and the word "deleted".

9. Deleting data

  • In the game: Profile > Account > Delete account removes the online account (the ID and the password) for good. A grown-up question comes first. The game on the phone is not changed: uninstall the game or clear its data to remove that too. Turning the leaderboards off stops any further sending.
  • On the web (you do not need to open the game or install it again): go to https://saudalsiyabi-dev.github.io/RayZero_privacy_policy/account_deletion.html or write to saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Squash data". Say which you want removed: (a) the online account, (b) the leaderboard scores, or both.
    • For (a) send the sign-in ID: the code that starts with "SQ-" (Profile > Account in the game).
    • For (b) send the "Online ID" shown at the bottom of the leaderboards screen.
    • We cannot find data without that ID, because the game knows no names or e-mail addresses. Please do not send passwords, names or photos.
    • We delete what the ID points to and reply within 30 days.
  • What we keep after a deletion: nothing else about the player.
  • Google and Unity keep what they collect under their own policies (see section 11). For the data Unity holds about our players we ask Unity to delete it when you ask us to.

10. Rights of parents and players

Depending on where you live (for example the EU and UK GDPR, or COPPA and state laws in the USA), you or your child may have the right to see, correct or delete data about you, to refuse more collection from a child, and to complain to a data protection authority. A parent or guardian can write to saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. We will not make a child's play depend on giving more data than the game needs.

11. Other companies

  • Google Play Billing, Google AdMob and Google Play services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity (In-App Purchasing, Authentication, Leaderboards): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple App Store (only if an iPhone version is added): Apple's own policy.

Data may be processed in countries other than yours, for example where Google and Unity run their servers. They describe their safeguards in their policies.

12. Security

Online traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in the game or by us. No system is perfectly safe, so we collect as little as possible.

13. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not show personalised ads. We do not send messages to players. We do not let players write anything that other players can read. We do not use the advertising ID.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we change the date above. For changes that matter we also say so in the store listing or in the game before they start.

15. Contact

Ray Zero, saudalsiyabi04@gmail.com, https://saudalsiyabi-dev.github.io/RayZero_privacy_policy/
