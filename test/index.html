Help us test Squash

A game by Ray Zero, coming soon to Google Play

Squash is a colourful tap-to-squash game for kids and families. Flies buzz across the screen and you tap them before they get away. Play through lots of levels, collect and dress up pets, and take on boss fights.

Join the test

What is in the game

  • Squash the flies on many levels, with combos and power-ups.
  • Brain Swarm: a quiz mode where you squash the fly that carries the right answer (sums, times tables, numbers, shapes and counting).
  • Pets to collect, level up and dress with hats, glasses, scarves and more.
  • Boss Rush, special abilities, different looks for the menus and a Royal Pass with rewards.
  • Made for children and families.

Join the test

Google asks every new game to be tried by real people for two weeks before it can be published. You can help in a few minutes:

  1. Fill in the form below with your name and your Gmail address (the Google account you use on your Android phone).
  2. We will email you an invitation with a link. Open it on your phone and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Squash from Google Play and keep it installed for 14 days. Please open it and play a bit now and then.
  4. Tell us anything that looks wrong or confusing. You can just reply to our email.

You need an Android phone and a Google (Gmail) account. Please sign up as an adult. Children can play on a parent's phone.
